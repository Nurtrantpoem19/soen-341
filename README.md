#CareerConnect
CareerConnect is a web-based platform designed to streamline and centralize the job search and recruitment process. It provides job seekers with tools to manage their profiles, track applications, and receive intelligent insights, while enabling recruiters to post job opportunities and manage talent pipelines effectively.

##Key Features
For Job Seekers
Profile & Resume Management: Create professional profiles, upload multiple resumes, and manage application documents.

Job Search & Discovery: Search and filter job listings based on location, industry, experience level, and work arrangements.

Application Tracking: Track submitted applications through dynamic status stages (Applied, Interview, Offered, Rejected).

Saved Jobs: Bookmark and organize target listings for future application.

Application History Dashboard: Centralized view of all current and historical job applications and progress metrics.

Notifications & Reminders: Automated alerts for upcoming application deadlines, interview dates, and status updates.

For Recruiters
Job Posting Management: Create, edit, publish, and archive job postings.

Candidate Tracking: Review applicant profiles, track candidate progression through hiring stages, and manage responses.
